A little about us

A café made for slowing down

Juniper & Roast began with a simple idea: make a comfortable neighborhood café where good things are made with care and everyone feels welcome.

It’s a place for students to open a book, nearby workers to take a proper lunch break, families to settle in after errands, and friends meeting over coffee.

We keep the food familiar, the coffee thoughtful, and the atmosphere easy. Whether you have ten minutes or the whole afternoon, there’s a seat for you.

What matters to us

The good things, done well.

Good Ingredients

We choose fresh, well-sourced ingredients and let them do their part in generous, satisfying food.

Thoughtful Coffee

We take the details seriously, from the beans to the brew, while keeping every cup approachable.

A Welcoming Table
